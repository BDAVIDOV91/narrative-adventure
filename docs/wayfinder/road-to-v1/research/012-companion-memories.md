## Do not claim

- "Belt of Venus", or any reference to Venus.
- That the Cassini Division is empty.
- That Mars's polar cap shows in a small telescope.
- That the Moon rises the same amount later every night.
- That the belt fades on a schedule.
- That Iapetus shows in a small telescope.
- Any date on a memory.
- Any number: minutes, widths, magnitudes or years.
