# Bulgarian folk constellation figures — settled 2026-09-09

Full detail is in `docs/sources.md` under "Bulgarian constellation names". This
file exists so a future audit does not re-run the 115k-token research pass.

## Source identified — do not re-search for it

доц. Ангел Бонов, _Митове и легенди за съзвездията_, София: „Наука и изкуство“,
1976, 281 с. Full text digitised at chitanka.info (scan 2011). His chapter titles
are „Орион (Ралица)“ and „Голяма мечка (Колата)“, so he independently attests
both folk **names**.

**Page numbers are NEEDS SOURCE** — the digitisation has no pagination. Do not
estimate one.

**He is not an ethnographic primary.** His 8-item bibliography contains no
Bulgarian ethnographic source at all — Покровский, Зигель, Гевелий, Кун, Акритас,
Буслович, and two Czech atlases. He reports the folk mapping without saying where
he got it.

## What is wrong and must not be re-proposed

- **χ in the рало is DISPUTED.** χ¹ Ori (HIP 27913) is Dec +20.28°, **21.9° from
  Alnilam** — further than the whole Rigel–Betelgeuse span (18.6°). Our own
  `constellation-lines.json` puts χ¹/χ² on Orion's **club** branch. Бонов's gloss
  "образуват десния крак" contradicts his own letters.
- **"вълк" = η UMa is NOT ATTESTED.** The wolf motif is real for both figures, but
  no source attaches it to η, and Бонов gives η = Коларят.
- **Бонов's Greek letters in these two passages are unreliable.** He writes
  „Мицар (ξ)“; Mizar is ζ UMa. Two wrong letters in the only two folk-mapping
  paragraphs. **Transcribe no Greek letter from these passages into game data.**

## What is good and usable

- **Mizar = Мечката / Alcor = кучето is Бонов-attested**, upgraded from
  PLAUSIBLE. The tale: a bear ate one ox, the youth harnessed the bear, it pulled
  sideways, „за това в съзвездието колата е разкривена“. **The dog barking at the
  bear _is_ Alcor** — the folk name is the naked-eye split, which is a genuine
  ADR 0005 pass for the `zoom-split-star` beat.
- Бонов tells the **wide** variant (a ~30° scene across three IAU constellations,
  including Сириус and Процион). The game tells the **narrow** one, per Вакарелски
  p. 413: eight stars, **which eight is not stated**.

## Terminology

**рало** = ard (symmetrical scratch plough), never плуг (mouldboard). **остен** =
ox-goad, not the plough handle. **Коларят** (η UMa per Бонов) collides with the
constellation **Колар** (Auriga), which Бонов also uses.
